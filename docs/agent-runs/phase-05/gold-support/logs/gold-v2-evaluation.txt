== uncertainty
  n = 230 labeled pairs, 5 labeled unsure and excluded from precision and recall; the metrics rest on that small sample.
  The sample is stratified by design (exact-id, hard-common-name, clear-non-match, about a third each), not random, so these are not population rates. Pooled precision and recall depend on the mix.
  Intervals are Wilson 95% intervals for a simple binomial, which ignores the stratification and the clustering of pairs by author.
  Recall is measured over candidate pairs only. Matches that blocking never proposed are not in the sample, so true recall is lower or equal.
  Gold labels come from one reviewer who saw an external suggestion; 14 labels differed from it. Agreement with a suggestion the reviewer saw may overstate independence.
  DBLP names carry numeric homonym suffixes, so many DBLP-DBLP labels are decided by DBLP's own disambiguation. Scores against those labels measure agreement with DBLP, not independent truth.
  Thresholds are provisional and hand-set. The sweep reads them off this sample only; a change chosen from it would be tuned to the evaluation set and needs a separate sample.
  This gold file has two passes. 153 labels are carried from the first pass, made with suggestions and not reviewed again; only 77 were reviewed blind. The pooled and per-stratum numbers mix the two. Read the by-pass section, and do not describe the carried labels as freshly reviewed.
