## Recommendation (infra-engineer): B
YARN MapReduce is certain only in B; B needs no IAM, security group or endpoint change and no new ledger version (Spark 4.0.5 already pinned, thin jar with -libjars and HADOOP_CLASSPATH avoids sbt-assembly); its cost is Spark packaging risk (JDK 17, archive, classpath). A is the simpler Spark story if the human accepts "MapReduce in local mode on an EMR node". Human decisions: A or B; whether single-node YARN satisfies the requirement or multi-node is needed (EFS small and suggested; S3A or HDFS rewrite large and cross-module); if B approve the Spark 4.0.5 tarball and its checksum as an artifact and choose thin with -libjars or fat (sbtAssembly ledger key); if A approve the 2 EC2 actions on the EMR service role and read the endpoint docs first.
