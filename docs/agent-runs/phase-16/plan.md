## Metrics per group and how each is produced

1. **Entity resolution** (offline): the human replays `EvaluationRun` on gold v3 unchanged (its report must equal `eval-v3-r3`; reproducibility evidence). The evaluation project adds per-path recall and a stratified bootstrap CI from `decisions.jsonl` and gold, and a tier S population view from the export (`nodes-researcher.linkStatus`, `endpointA/BUnresolved` rates). Threats: gold built on the Phase 4 subset, not tier S; about 12 suspected label errors (metrics shown with and without them); LLM adjudication is a stub.
2. **Graph correctness** (offline): new `GraphChecks` over A's export: referential integrity (edge endpoints in `nodes-researcher`; `edge-support` and `edges-authored` work references in `nodes-work`), duplicate rate per id column, orphan rate (researchers or works with no authored edge), coauthor-edge support rate (at least one support row, and `jointPublicationCount` equals the support count), path-evidence hops against `edges-coauthored` (P15-28), provenance completeness (`runId` equals `sourceRunId`), reconciliation with the Neptune load counts (32,225 vertices, 135,788 edges). Sampled provenance audit: 50 random edges traced from edge to supporting works to authored edges to source record ids; the human checks 10. Known-graph tests: the cited suites plus counts from the human's `sbt clean check` log.
3. **Retrieval** (run G; laptop; stub synthesis; Titan and S3 Vectors): per question and mode (graph, vector, fused): Recall@K and Precision@K (K = 2 as deployed, plus 5 and 10 through a direct `search`), MRR, nDCG@K with graded labels; bootstrap CIs; two repeats. OpenSearch: not built, stated.
4. **Answer quality** (run H; laptop; fused mode; Haiku after the WO-3 fix): an independent `AnswerAudit` in the evaluation project (not the system's verifier): structural claim support (every claim cites evidence ids present in the bundle); coauthor-edge, graph-path and supporting-publication validity against the export; identifier and numeric accuracy (ids and numbers in claims and evidence against the tables, tolerance stated); contradiction rate (claims whose ids or numbers conflict with the export). The system verifier's own CONFLICTING and UNSUPPORTED verdicts are reported separately as self-reported. Abstention: recall on the 8 unanswerable, false-abstention rate on answerable, reason-class match. Plus the human rating.
5. **Performance and elasticity**: reuse `experiments/RESULTS.md` (no re-run); add bootstrap CIs for D's p50 and p95 per level from `derived-requests/requests.jsonl` (small option in `derive_latency.py`, human-run); Haiku per-call latency and tokens from H (laptop view); billed-cost reconciliation of Phase 15.
6. **Agentic factory**: `FactoryCounts` (Scala, evaluation project) over `docs/agent-runs/phase-01` to `phase-15`, with per-phase parsing rules and an explicit mapping table where formats drift: first-pass acceptance by agent (first review verdict per work order), BLOCKED-ON events, ownership violations caught (findings tagged authority or ownership), findings by normalised severity, repair rounds per phase, manual interventions (human-command sections and human rulings), guard or hook refusals (prose mentions; partial, stated), substantial human rewrites (a human-annotated list, the only manual input). Every count is printed with the matched file and line so the human can audit it.
7. **Reproducibility**: clean-checkout build (the human clones into a temporary directory and runs `sbt clean check`); replay of the bounded local sample pipeline and of `EvaluationRun`, compared by checksum; deployment from an approved plan cited from `docs/releases/phase-14/` and `phase-15/README.md` (no new deploy); B to F derived tables regenerated from raw and compared with the committed `SHA256SUMS` (count of identical and differing files).

### Human rating rubric (D7)

Sample: 20 Haiku answers, stratified (2 or 3 per answerable stratum, 3 abstentions, 2 free-form), shown with the question and the evidence bundle and without any metric. Per answer (about 3 minutes):

- Usefulness 1 to 5 (1 does not answer; 3 answers partly or needs digging; 5 answers fully and is directly usable).
- Clarity 1 to 5 (1 confusing; 3 understandable with effort; 5 clear, concise, well ordered).
- Supported (yes or no): every statement is backed by the shown evidence.
- Abstention appropriate (yes, no, n.a.).
- Note (optional).

Form: a CSV the human fills; frozen with sha256 before any summary. Single rater, so no inter-rater agreement (threat stated).
