## What it does
- `AnswerEval --diagnostic` (H-4): at most 4 answer-model calls, first answerable question of each of up to four strata, or exactly the questions named by `--question-ids A,B` (order kept). Writes `diagnostic.jsonl` (appended and flushed per question: stratum, abstention fields, tokens and every model attempt with `stopReason`, rejection codes, output tokens and, when recorded, the text-free measures `replyChars`, `claimsStarted`, `repeatRatio`, `evidenceItems`, `rankingEntries`, `rankedTotal`, `cutoffTies`, `tieAtCutoff` and `retryDecision`), its `RAW-SHA256SUMS`, and `diagnostic-summary.json` (also `retryDecisions` counts and `attemptMeasures`, one text-free entry per attempt). The same measures are kept in each attempt of `answers.jsonl`. No question text and no answer text. Console: `DIAGNOSTIC calls=<n> stopReasons=<reason:count,...> maxTokens=<n>`.
- `AnswerEval` full: every question once in fused mode (primary pass), then a seeded stratified repeat of up to 20 answerable, answered questions (temperature 0, answer variability). Appends `answers.jsonl` as each answer finishes (claims, evidence, the system verifier's own report, abstention fields, model attempts, tokens, latency; flushed to the operating system at once, so a kill keeps the answers made so far), then writes `RAW-SHA256SUMS` over it before any summary; then `model-attempts-summary.json`, `model-calls.jsonl`, `answer-summary.json`, and a stratified 20-answer `rating-sample.csv` (columns `sampleId, question, answer, evidence, usefulness_1to5, clarity_1to5, supported_yes_no, abstention_appropriate_yes_no_na, note`; no question id, because the ids carry the stratum, and no stratum or metric) with its `rating-sample-key.json` kept apart (it maps `sampleId` to the question id). `answers.jsonl` and the CSV are the only files that hold question and answer text.
- `AnswerAudit` (no AWS, a separate step on the frozen `answers.jsonl`): independent of the system verifier. Structural claim support; which claims are factual is decided by the audit (a claim the verifier calls a `definition` or `abstention` is still audited when its text states an identifier or a number or it cites deterministic evidence; the summary counts `reclassifiedFactualByAudit` and `excludedByVerifierKind`); coauthor-edge, path-hop and supporting-publication validity against A's export; identifier and numeric accuracy (tolerance 0.01 absolute plus 0.001 relative, stated in the output); contradiction rate; the verifier's verdicts reported apart as self-reported; abstention recall, false-abstention rate and reason-code match against `expectedCodes`; repeat variability. Spelled-out numbers are not read (a stated limit).

## Stops (checked BEFORE each call)
Haiku call cap 460 (decisions P16-3 and P16-29), Titan call cap 1000, USD stop 3.00 (binding; raised from 2.00 by decision P16-33 because the synthesis `maxOutputTokens` is now 4096, P16-32; a full synthesis call is about 0.027 USD: input about 7k tokens x 1.00 per million plus 4096 x 5.00 per million): spend so far plus the worst case of the next call (input = request and system characters over four, output = `maxOutputTokens`) must stay within the ceiling; spend is metered from the Converse usage tokens, and a failed call or a reply flagged as estimated is charged at the worst case. Every USD figure is an estimate at UNVERIFIED rates, not a bound (Titan is characters over four, so spend can pass the ceiling by the error of one call). A refused call raises no request; synthesis then reports `synthesis-failed`. Exit codes: 0 complete, 1 failed, 2 usage, 3 halted. An answer in which the guard refused a call is marked `costStopped` and is excluded from the audit and the sample; an answer whose last call finished and pushed spend past the ceiling is a finished answer (the next call is refused).

Why 460: the planner classifier misses the rules for free-form and out-of-scope questions and then calls Haiku through `StructuredCall` (up to 3 attempts), and synthesis makes up to 3 attempts, so the worst case is 6 calls per answer. 76 answers (56 primary plus 20 repeat) x 6 = 456, plus 4 slack = 460. The manifest (`MAX_TASKS 460`), `run.sh` (`HAIKU_MAX_CALLS=460`, and it refuses a `MAX_TASKS` below it) and this README agree. The manifest stays valid under `scripts/run-experiment.sh` (tier S caps 90 min, 64, 20000; projected spend 0.80 x 90 / 60 = 1.20 under 3.00; the spend ceiling 3.00 is within the `--cost-ceiling-usd 20` of the command, tier S has no cost cap of its own; experiment id H accepted).

Wall clock: `run.sh` passes an absolute deadline, `MAX_MINUTES` (90) minus 10 minutes after the start of `run.sh`. sbt start, compile and the graph load (about 6 GB; duration unmeasured until the first live run) are inside that window. The deadline is checked between answers, so the run can pass it by one answer: at most 6 Haiku calls at the 40 s call deadline (4 minutes) plus 2 Titan embeds at the 60 s attempt deadline with no SDK retry (2 minutes), then the closing writes (under a minute); 10 minutes is the margin. A dependency that hangs past it is cut by the kill at `MAX_MINUTES`; the flushed `answers.jsonl` keeps the answers made so far (no `RAW-SHA256SUMS`, summaries, rating sample or seal in that case). If the graph load alone eats the window, nothing is answered and the run reports `HALTED` with `halt=wall-clock` and zero records.

If Billing headroom is under 3.40 USD for G, H and the diagnostic (0.25 + 3.00 + 0.15; was 2.40 at the H ceiling of 2.00), change the ceiling in the manifest and in `run.sh` together (new digest, new approval record); the repeat is dropped first (`REPEAT_COUNT=0` in `run.sh`).

## H cost if Haiku ignores the ranking claim cap (review 10 finding 8)
Synthesis 1.2.0 rule 6 limits a ranking reply to entries + 2 claims, and a completed reply over that cap is retried up to 3 times (P16-41). If Haiku ignores the rule, each question whose evidence has a ranking fact costs 3 synthesis calls instead of 1. Rule 6 applies by evidence (`Synthesis.scala:112-113`, `shape.rankingEntries`), not by stratum, so the count is the 10 ranking-stratum questions plus the 2 answerable free-form ranking questions (`labels-v1.jsonl` lines 53 and 54, operation `ranking`) = 12: 12 x 2 extra calls x about 0.0275 USD = about 0.66 USD over the 2.09 USD projection (about 2.75 USD). Whether lines 53-54 and a bridge-analysis question really produce a ranking fact depends on the planner (not verified). Only an answer whose 3 attempts all fail abstains `synthesis-failed` and is left out of the repeat pass. A reply rejected once or twice and then accepted counts as answered, so it can be picked for the repeat pass (`AnswerEval.scala:285-296`) and cost up to 3 calls again. The repeat pass takes 20 questions round-robin over the strata of the eligible questions (`Subsets.stratified`, one per stratum in turn): with about 7 answerable strata (6 generated plus free-form) that is about 3 per stratum, so about 3 ranking-stratum questions (range 2-3 of 10) plus 0-2 of the 2 free-form ranking questions (among 3 free-form picked of 6), about 4 ranking questions (range 2-5; an estimate, the real strata sizes and the seed's order decide it). 4 x 2 extra calls x about 0.0275 USD = about 0.22 USD, so about 2.75 + 0.22 = about 2.97 USD in the worst case, under the 3.00 ceiling with headroom of only about 0.03 USD. At the upper bound of 5 ranking questions the repeat adds about 0.275 USD, about 3.03 USD in total, which is over the ceiling: the USD stop is checked before each call and ends the run, so the repeat pass is cut and spend cannot exceed 3.00 USD. The 460 call cap already counts 3 synthesis attempts per answer. Not measured: Haiku compliance with rule 6.

## Limits
- Laptop-to-Bedrock latency, not in-VPC; the graph port is `InMemoryNeptune`.
- The audit and the labels share A's export, so they check consistency with the graph, not real-world truth.
- Single human rater for the rating sample, no inter-rater agreement.
- The `out-of-scope` abstention mapping is the stub's behaviour; a real model may name an operation instead, and a different code is a finding (`benchmark/README.md`).
- A cost stop ends the run early; the records made before it stay and the answer whose call was refused is excluded.
