## Experiment E and the retrieval data

- L8 (P15-38). A's export holds `path-evidence` with 42 hub-pair rows (default `--auto-pair-top 4`: 6 pairs per window times 7 windows). This is a stated design limit, not a defect. Path queries in E therefore go through Neptune (graph-only); the vector and fused modes cannot answer arbitrary path questions from the export. The live read path also no longer checks path-evidence hop edges against `edges-coauthored` (P15-28); that check is deferred to the Phase 16 graph-correctness evaluation.
- L9 (P15-44, D1 option 2). E is MIXED provenance: the vectors come from the earlier ingest run; the graph and read models come from A (`run:v1:3cfb57b1-abd1-4913-8e53-da477d8d6027`). The earlier ingest `sourceRunId` should be recorded beside A's; it is not in my sources (NOT STATED). `GAP_NOTE_TRUE_COUNT=0` (no response carried a true `vector_gap_note`). Cross-mode comparisons mix data of two runs and must not be read as one coherent snapshot. E also has the usual cache effect of interleaved order (a cache filled by one mode's request can help the next mode's request for the same question; the rotation balances it, it does not remove it), one client at low load (retrieval cost, not behavior under load), and a stub synthesis step that makes synthesis time and answer content equal in nature across modes.
- L12 (P15-45, finding 4 and deviation 8). The human deleted vectors in six batches outside the runbook (C's 2,000 keys were removed from three indexes) and purged the embedding DLQ twice. E ran before the deletion; any vector-mode run after it lacks the old vectors for those keys. E's data stands as recorded (mixed provenance), but E cannot be rerun to the same state.
